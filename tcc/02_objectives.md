# 2 OBJECTIVES

## 2.1 General objective

        The general objective of this study was to assess the relative performance of Random Forest and LightGBM models with respect to ARIMA model in forecasting how continuously changing exchange rates affect bilateral trade flows between the United States, Canada, Mexico, and Brazil with granular level insights about sectoral movement and macroeconomic effects.

## 2.2 Specific objectives

        The specific objectives were:

        a) To pinpoint which sectors are most vulnerable to exchange rate changes;

        b) To determine the impact of these swings across different time horizons;

        c) To evaluate the predictive power of each model;

        d) To examine the effect of macroeconomic factors on the volatility of trade flows.
